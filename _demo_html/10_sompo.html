Welcome to the CGA M365 Copilot Immersion

Two parts, eight activities, bilingual prompts, ready in 90 minutes.

90 min All levels Bilingual EN and ID Copilot Chat, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Planner

This session takes the transformation office at Contoso General Assurance Indonesia through practical Copilot patterns for the PMO and for business process re-engineering. Part A covers project intake, steering committee decks, RAID logs and stakeholder updates across the Motor and Property Claims Platform Modernisation, the Marine Cargo Digital Onboarding, the Bancassurance Distribution Integration, the Sharia Product Launch and the Auto Manufacturer Partnership API rollout. Part B covers as-is process capture, bottleneck analysis, SOP rewrites and change communications for motor, property fire and marine cargo claims, plus commercial property underwriting with an earthquake hazard callout.

BS

Budi Santoso

Chief Transformation Officer, Contoso General Assurance Indonesia

Budi is CTO at CGA Indonesia, a general and non-life insurer. His remit spans three divisions: Personal (motor, property, health, personal accident, travel), Corporate (property and fire, liability, marine cargo, engineering, fleet and commercial vehicle, employee benefits) and Specialty (sharia and earthquake). His distribution mix reaches customers through brokers, agents, bancassurance partners, leasing partners and auto manufacturer channels. He sponsors five active programmes: the Motor and Property Claims Platform Modernisation, the Marine Cargo Digital Onboarding, the Bancassurance Distribution Integration, the Sharia Product Launch, and the Auto Manufacturer Partnership API rollout. Every activity in this immersion is written from Budi's point of view or from a member of his team.

GCSE, write better prompts

Great prompts share four ingredients. Use the GCSE mnemonic to make sure every prompt has all four. Inside Part A and Part B, toggle GCSE colour coded at the top of the tab to see every prompt broken into these four colours.

LetterStands forWhat it doesExample fragment
G GoalWhat you want Copilot to doThe verb, the outcome, the deliverable."Produce a one page steering committee pre-read..."
C ContextWho you are, what situationRole, audience, purpose, constraints."I am the CTO at CGA Indonesia, chairing the Motor and Property Claims Platform Modernisation steering committee..."
S SourceWhere to ground the answerFiles, sheets, URLs, jurisdictions, or "use your training" if none."Use the attached 01_CGA_ProjectStatusReport.docx"
E ExpectationHow the output should lookLength, format, tone, sections, language."Bahasa Indonesia, 200 words, three bullets per section, professional and calm"

Tips and prompts

Quick reference for after the session.

Prompt hygiene Common patterns What Copilot will not do

✍ Prompt hygiene for CGA

  • Start with role. "I am the PMO Lead / Head of Operations / underwriter at Contoso General Assurance Indonesia". This alone lifts quality.
  • Name the audience. "steering committee", "regional claims team", "underwriters, 1 to 15 years experience". Copilot calibrates tone accordingly.
  • Say the language. "Bahasa Indonesia formal" or "English, calm and factual". Do not assume.
  • Set the length. "300 words", "one page", "12 bullet points". Vague length equals vague output.
  • Cite the source. "Use the attached file 03_CGA_RAID_Log.xlsx", or "Use publicly available AAJI/AAUI data" if grounding externally.

🔁 Common patterns

  • Turn a doc into a deck. Copilot Chat, "Create a presentation from 02_CGA_SteeringCommitteeBrief.docx".
  • Turn a transcript into a process map. Copilot in Word, ask for an as-is table with owners and hand-offs.
  • Turn a survey into a to-be design. Copilot Chat with the xlsx, ask for bottlenecks plus KPIs plus a Mermaid diagram.
  • Turn a policy manual into an SOP. Prompt-in-doc inside the manual, request a new SOP grounded on a specific section.
  • Turn a chat draft into a warm email. Copy the draft into Outlook, use Coaching by Copilot to rewrite the tone.
What Copilot will not do for CGA. Approve a claim, bind a policy, price a risk, replace an underwriter's judgement, or read data outside your Microsoft 365 tenant unless you connect it. Copilot accelerates the humans; it does not replace them.
